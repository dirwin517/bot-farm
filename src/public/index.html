Open a folder

A workspace is a folder with its opencode config. BotFarm keeps its agents and pipelines in a botfarm/ folder at its root, one file each (<id>.agent.botfarm.yml, <id>.pipeline.botfarm.yml), and its own tools in botfarm/mcps/. They are created if missing.

Start a workstream

A split stage turns the list the stage before it hands over into one card per item. This many bots of that kind take them at the same time, in the same worktree; the next stage waits for all of them.

Add a repo to this workstream

It gets its own worktree on this workstream's branch, inside the workstream's worktree, and the bots are told where it is. A repo from outside the workspace is mounted under its folder name.

Model prices

opencode reports a cost only when the provider returns one (Bedrock does not), so BotFarm prices tokens itself. USD per million tokens; cache reads are charged at a tenth of input, cache writes at 1.25×. Saved in ~/.botfarm/config.json.

Restart this workstream

Stages before it keep their handoffs. This one and everything after go back to waiting their turn.

Apply the work to your repos

Each repo's changes (edits, new files, and anything committed on the branch) become a patch, applied to your real checkout on whatever branch it is on — unstaged and uncommitted, ready to review in your IDE. BotFarm's own files are left out. The .patch files are kept, so you can do this again later.
